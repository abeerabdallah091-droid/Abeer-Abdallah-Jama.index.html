# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Somali women aged 18–40, based in Somalia and the diaspora. They are health-conscious and beauty-aware, seeking supplements that feel trustworthy and premium — not cheap or generic. They shop on mobile, discover through Instagram and TikTok, and make purchase decisions based on brand feel and social proof as much as product specs.

## Product Purpose

Ridwa Nutrition Center sells beauty and wellness supplements — primarily Marine Collagen — to women who want to look and feel their best. The site exists to build confidence in the brand, showcase products, and convert visitors into buyers. Success is a customer who trusts the brand enough to purchase on first visit.

## Brand Personality

Confident, warm, elevated. Science meets beauty. Rooted in the Somali community but aspirational in aesthetic. The brand should feel like a premium beauty brand that happens to be yours — not a foreign import, not a generic supplement company.

## Anti-references

- Generic pharmacy: cold, clinical, white/blue medical aesthetic — this is exactly what Ridwa is not.
- Cheap dropship store: overcrowded layouts, excessive discount badges, no visual identity.
- Plain blog: no design ambition, just text on a page.
- Western fitness brand: aggressive, gym-bro energy, loud neon colors — wrong audience, wrong tone.

## Design Principles

1. **Science meets warmth.** The brand claims science-backed benefits but the audience wants to feel good, not educated. Lead with beauty and confidence; support with credibility.
2. **Premium without distance.** High-end aesthetics that still feel accessible and community-rooted. Not cold luxury — warm luxury.
3. **Mobile-first, beauty-first.** This audience discovers on Instagram and TikTok. Every section should look stunning on a phone screen.
4. **Trust through clarity.** No clutter, no tricks, no overwhelm. A clean site is a confident site. Confidence builds trust.
5. **Color carries the brand.** The green/gold palette is distinctive. Use it boldly — don't dilute it into generic neutrals.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Strong color contrast for text over colored backgrounds. No reliance on color alone to convey meaning. Consider users on slower mobile connections in Somalia — minimize render-blocking assets.
